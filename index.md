---
title: MotionForge
---

MotionForge turns plain-language prompts into animation inside the Unreal Editor. It runs NVIDIA's open
Kimodo motion model on your own machine, then lets you trim, pose, export and retarget the result onto your
character.

## Guides

- **[Installation Guide](installation-guide.md)**: installing the plugin and downloading the model weights
- **[User Guide](user-guide.md)**: the Recipe editor, writing prompts, posing, exporting and retargeting
- **[Prompting Guide](prompting-guide.md)**: prompt patterns that work, by kind of motion, with durations and settings
- **[Characters Guide](characters-guide.md)**: MetaHumans, Character Creator, Mixamo and your own characters
- **[MetaHuman Guide](metahuman-guide.md)**: a MetaHuman from retarget to playable: Level Target, clean-up, Merge Body + Garments
- **[Sequencer Guide](sequencer-guide.md)**: the MotionForge track, generating between poses and Control Rig keys

The same guides ship inside the plugin (`MotionForge/Docs/`) and open from the **Guide** button in the Recipe
editor.

## Requirements

- Unreal Engine 5.8, Windows (editor-only; exported animations work on every platform)
- About 16 GB of disk for the model weights, downloaded once by the in-editor Setup Wizard
